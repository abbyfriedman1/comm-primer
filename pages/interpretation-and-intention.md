## The Fourth Intention

Intentio algorithmi translates to “intention of the algorithm.” In other words, the reasoning and way that the software selects, orders, and produces content. AI plays a role in this action by collecting and analyzing users’ data across social platforms and websites, enabling it to produce a customized feed based on those findings.

This term addresses the influence of the algorithm by demonstrating the way that the algorithm can manipulate and alter one’s feed. Content is tailored to the individual, and changes the message to adapt based on what the user frequently  views and interacts with. AI can do a deep dive to see all types of information such as who someone follows, the content they click on the most, their most used platforms, etc.

Based on a user's activity, it will find similar content it thinks the user will find interesting or relevant. This gives AI a substantial role in the communication process as a whole, because it has the ability to control the viewers' feed content on a daily basis.

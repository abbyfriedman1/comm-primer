There are several steps you can take to help reduce the influence that algorithmic feeds may have on you.

One key aspect is to always be aware of persuasion and other strategies used by different accounts and websites to skew or alter your perspective. Not everything in your feed is bad by any means, but it’s crucial to know the ways and tactics used to influence users. 

Being mindful of the apps and websites you use, and their privacy settings can be another way to help take precaution against algorithmic feeds. By being more informed of the platforms you’re using, you can understand how they work and what type of data they collect from you, so you can then decide if you want to use it.

Ultimately, the simplest way to reduce the negative impacts of the algorithmic feeds is to limit your social media use.

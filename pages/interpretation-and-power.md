In regards to Stuart Hall's theory on encoding and decoding,  Mambrol 2020 states that, “According to this model, the sender creates the message and fixes its meaning, which is then communicated directly and transparently to the recipient.” Hall believed that meaning derived from “every moment in a circuit”, and that it’s produced from both the sender and receiver.

This comes into play with how algorithmic feeds control meaning in today’s world. These feeds have a significant influence on what people consume, which in response can impact the way people make meaning of the things they're viewing. This starts to alter their view of the world and the things taking place around them, because they are viewing things from their personally curated feed, that is tailored to their interests and preferences.

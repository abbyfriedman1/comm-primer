## Introduction

This project explores how classic and contemporary communication frameworks can make everyday misunderstandings easier to identify and solve, especially when artificial intelligence systems now mediate so much of human interaction. The specific topic that is explored is the effect of algorithmic feeds and how they influence and shape viewers' beliefs.

With the rapid advancements in technology and AI today, there’s been an increase with the customizability in apps. Artificial intelligence plays a large role in the way that algorithmic feeds are individualized to the user, across media platforms. Each user’s feed is personalized to their interest, based on data collected through their device. This process affects everyone who uses social media or any other online platform, and it’s something that happens every day. Persuasive, skewed, and false information can all show up in a user’s feed, which can influence the way they think or see the world.

AI plays a significant role in the process of creating algorithmic feeds. “Machine-learning models analyze content, account history and behavioral patterns to identify and flag inaccurate posts—often before human moderators ever see them” (Lanman, 2026). This demonstrates how AI can be used to track users' activity across platforms, influencing the content they see. These influences can impact us both personally and professionally.

## What this site is for

- Turning abstract theory into everyday guidance.
- Making hidden assumptions in communication visible.
- Helping readers reason through communication breakdowns involving AI tools and platforms.

## Site structure

Each section applies a specific theoretical lens to 21st-century communication problems, with real-life examples, case studies, and analysis.

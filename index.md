## AI Everywhere- A Primer for Personal and Professional Use
## Introduction

This project explores how classic and contemporary communication frameworks can make everyday misunderstandings easier to identify and solve, especially when artificial intelligence systems. The specific topic that will be focused on is the effects of algorithmic feeds and how they influence and shape our beliefs.

With the rapid advancements in technology and AI today, there’s been an increase in the customizability of apps. Artificial intelligence plays a large role in the way that algorithmic feeds are individualized to the user, across different media platforms. Each user’s feed is personalized to their interest, based on data collected through their device. This proces affects everyone who uses social media or any other online platform, and it’s something that happens every day. Persuasive, skewed, and false information can all show up in a user’s feed, which can lead to shaping their beliefs. The more a user views a type of  content, the more likely it is for similar content to continue popping up in their feed.

AI plays a significant role in the process of creating algorithmic feeds. “Machine-learning models analyze content, account history and behavioral patterns to identify and flag inaccurate posts—often before human moderators ever see them” (Lanman, 2026). This demonstrates how AI can be used to track users' activity across platforms, influencing the content they see. This influence can impact us both personally and professionally.


## What This Site Is For

- Turning abstract theory into everyday guidance.
- Making hidden assumptions in communication visible.
- Helping readers reason through communication breakdowns involving AI tools and platforms.

## Site Structure

Each section applies a specific theoretical lens to 21st-century communication problems, with real-life examples, case studies, and analysis.
